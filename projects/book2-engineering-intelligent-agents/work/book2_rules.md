BOOK-SPECIFIC RULES for this book. Where they differ from the rules above, these win.

The book is "Engineering Intelligent Agents: Designing, Orchestrating, Optimizing, and Deploying Agentic AI Systems", 15 chapters. For each chapter I will send you its outline with the exact section titles.

1. SECTIONS FOLLOW MY OUTLINE EXACTLY. Every chapter has exactly the sections in its outline, with the same numbers and the same titles, in the same order. Chapters have between 6 and 9 sections, so the "maximum 8 sections" and "X.8 is the Chapter Summary" rules do NOT apply to this book. Do not add, drop, merge, split, rename or reorder sections.

2. CHAPTER SUMMARY. After the last outline section, every chapter ends with one more section, the Chapter Summary, numbered next. Example: Chapter 2 has sections 2.1 to 2.6, so the summary is "## 2.7  Chapter Summary". Chapter 3 has 3.1 to 3.9, so it is "## 3.10  Chapter Summary". All other summary rules stay the same.

3. LENGTH. The length rule above applies to every content section of the chapter, not only X.1 to X.6. Each content section is about 1,500 words. When I send the concluding prompt, write the remaining sections concisely as described.

4. FIGURES. At most 5 to 6 figures per chapter, about one per content section, never more than 2 in one section and none in the Chapter Summary. Every figure in a different visual style.

5. NUMBERING. Figure numbers AND table numbers run continuously through the whole book (the first table of Chapter 2 continues from the last table of Chapter 1). Equations are NOT numbered: never write "Equation (N)" or "Eq. N". Refer to an equation by position instead ("the equation above", "the preceding expression").

6. WRITING STYLE in all prose:
- American English spelling (organization, analyze, behavior, modeling, optimize, toward).
- No first person and no direct address: never use I, we, our, us, you, your or "let us". Write in the third person ("the agent", "this chapter", "engineers").
- No em dashes or en dashes. Use commas or separate sentences, and write number ranges as "2 to 6".
- No contractions (it is, does not, cannot). Possessives such as "the agent's" are fine.
- No colons or semicolons in prose. Split into two sentences or join with a conjunction. Colons are fine only in headings, captions and math.

7. NO REPETITION across the whole book, as stated above. When a later section needs an earlier idea, refer back to it by section number ("see Section 2.3") and add only what is new.

Reply only with "Understood." and wait for my next instruction. Do not start writing any chapter yet.
